## Building software around real business work

SAHARA Labs is a Nepal-based product studio building focused business systems for organizations that need speed, correctness, clear controls, and trustworthy records.

Our products are developed alongside practical workflows in retail, wholesale, manufacturing, accounting, and hospitality. That keeps our design grounded in how people actually sell, purchase, reconcile, count stock, approve work, and close the day.

## Flagship product: SAHARA CORE

**SAHARA CORE is currently in active development.** It is an original, fully web-based accounting, inventory, and business operating system designed around:

- desktop-class, keyboard-first workflows;
- rigorous double-entry accounting and report reconciliation;
- inventory, bill-wise accounting, banking, tax, and audit workflows;
- secure multi-company operation and role-based controls;
- Nepal-ready business requirements with an extensible multi-country architecture;
- SaaS and locally managed deployment models.

We treat a product decision, an implementation, a verification result, and a release as different states. Planned or partially tested work is never presented as shipped.

## What guides our engineering

- **Correctness before decoration** — financial and inventory results must reconcile.
- **Auditability by design** — important business actions should be attributable and explainable.
- **Fast professional workflows** — keyboard efficiency and information density matter.
- **Secure boundaries** — permissions, company isolation, and trusted server-side validation are foundational.
- **Evidence over claims** — tests, runtime behavior, and reproducible proof determine product status.
- **Practical adaptability** — Nepal-first workflow knowledge, without limiting the architecture to one country.
